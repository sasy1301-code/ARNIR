Industries

Certified to the standard
that flies.

Every ARNIR factory is certified to AS9100D – the aerospace quality standard – so airborne programs are built on the same documented process, traceability and test discipline as our defense work.

Our approach

A documented process, from requirement to certification.

Our engineering team carries every design through each key step: requirements gathering and specification development, material selection, cable architecture and electrical design, tooling and prototyping, testing – through to obtaining the necessary certifications for safety and performance.

This structured approach is the reason every cable, connector and harness reaches mass production already proven.

AS9100D is the aerospace industry’s quality management standard. ARNIR holds it alongside ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485:2016.

What we build

Interconnect built to take the load.

  • Custom wire harnesses and cable assemblies
  • Harsh-environment connectors – vibration, temperature and humidity
  • Composite material housings and insulator connectors
  • High-density, multi-pin round and rectangular connectors
  • PCB assembly with SPI, AOI and X-ray inspection
  • System assembly and electro-mechanical integration
