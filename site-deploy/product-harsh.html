Products

For places a standard
connector will not survive.

Connectors engineered for vibration, temperature extremes and moisture – including composite material housings and insulator connectors, and disposable variants where a clean interface is required.

Why they hold

The environment is part of the specification.

From the first consultation we document the environmental conditions the connector will face alongside the electrical requirements and mechanical strength – then select conductors, insulation and housing materials against that specification rather than against a catalogue.

Testing follows, to confirm the assembly meets all specified operational requirements and industry standards before it is released to production.
