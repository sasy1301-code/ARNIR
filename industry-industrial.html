Industries

The wiring behind
the production floor.

Industrial power cables, molded cables, and industrial wire harnesses produced in our factories are installed and used all around the world.

Our approach

One supplier – from the cable to the finished system.

The full assembled industrial applications are manufactured in ARNIR with stringent quality control measures to ensure that every product we produce meets the highest standards of performance and durability.

With the 2022 acquisition of FTK Solutions Ltd. and the addition of another 2,000 SQM of production floor, the group delivers complete turnkey solutions in the military and industrial fields.

The factory in Bangalore has 3,500 square meters of production floor and specializes in wire harnesses production, cable assemblies, electrical looms, cabinet wiring, and electromechanical integrations.

What we build

Wiring, cabinets and integration.

  • Industrial power cables
  • Molded cables
  • Industrial wire harnesses and electrical looms
  • Cabinet wiring
  • Electro-mechanical integrations
  • Complete turnkey solutions and finished systems
