Industries we serve

Built for environments
that don’t forgive failure.

Medical, military, industrial and communication – three worlds with one shared requirement: the interconnect has to work, every time, for as long as the product lives.

Medical

ARNIR specializes in the production of a wide range of medical cables, catheters, pre-amps, system cables, and connectors. ARNIR products are used worldwide in hospitals, operating rooms, emergency rooms, and homes.

More on medical

Aerospace & Aviation

With more than 30 years of experience, ARNIR is a well-known and deeply involved figure in the Israeli Aerospace & Aviation industry. We produce a wide range of cables, systems and custom-made connectors.

More on aerospace & aviation

Industrial and Communication

A wide range of RF cable and connector assemblies, including RG, micro coax and semi-flex, alongside industrial power cables, molded cables and wire harnesses installed all around the world.
