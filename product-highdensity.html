Products

More contacts,
same footprint.

High-density, multi-pin round and rectangular connectors – designed and produced in cooperation with Kenpol, and tailored to the varied demands of today’s fast-paced industries.

The family

Round and rectangular, built to the application.

Both round and rectangular formats are produced, with the contact arrangement worked out against the application: signal count, current, spacing and the mating environment the connector will live in.

By embracing advanced materials, technologies and precision machining, we provide solutions and support from design and prototyping to full-scale production – for military, medical, telecommunications and industrial applications alike.
