We offer a wide range of RF cable and connector assemblies, including RG, micro coax, semi-flex and other special custom-made cables and connectors.

Our approach

Performance and durability, in every unit that leaves the floor.

The full assembled telecommunication applications are manufactured in ARNIR with stringent quality control measures to ensure that every product we produce meets the highest standards of performance and durability.

ARNIR specializes in the production of custom cable and harness assemblies for the military, medical, and telecommunication industries. With five factories in Israel, Thailand, and India, and more than 300 operators, we excel in high-mix, low-volume, and mass production.

ARNIR provides PCB assembly for the military, medical, and telecommunication industries. Our team utilizes technology and processes to ensure that every PCB assembly we produce meets the highest standards.

What we build

RG, micro-coax and semi-flex assemblies.

  • RF cable assemblies – RG, micro coax and semi-flex
  • Special custom-made cables and connectors
  • RF connectors
  • PCB assembly (PCBA) for telecommunication applications
  • Full assembly of finished telecommunication applications
