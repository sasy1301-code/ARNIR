Industries

RF assemblies installed
all around the world.

A wide range of RF cable and connector assemblies – RG, micro-coax, semi-flex and other special custom-made cables and connectors – produced in our factories and installed across the telecommunications sector worldwide.

Our approach

Performance and durability, in every unit that leaves the floor.

Fully assembled telecommunication applications are manufactured at ARNIR with stringent quality control measures, to ensure that every product we produce meets the highest standards of performance and durability.

RF assemblies are produced alongside our industrial power cables, molded cables and wire harnesses – so a single supplier covers the whole interconnect chain of the site.

ARNIR has been designing and producing connectors in cooperation with Kenpol for more than 15 years – RF connectors included.

What we build

RG, micro-coax and semi-flex assemblies.

  • RF cable assemblies – RG, micro-coax and semi-flex
  • Special custom-made cables and connectors
  • RF connectors
  • Wire harnesses and molded cables for communication infrastructure
  • PCB assembly (PCBA) for telecommunication applications
  • Full assembly of finished telecommunication applications
