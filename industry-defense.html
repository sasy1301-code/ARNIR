With more than 30 years of experience, ARNIR is well-known and deeply involved in the aerospace industry.

Our experience

Readiness and effectiveness, under any condition.

We take pride in our extensive history and experience, which have positioned us as leaders in this critical sector.

We produce a wide range of cables, systems and custom-made connectors for different applications, with reliable, state-of-the-art solutions that ensure their readiness and effectiveness.

Our team is continually researching and developing to stay ahead of the curve and remain at the forefront of aerospace innovation.

What we build

From a single connector to a complete system.

  • Custom cables and wire harnesses for critical applications
  • Harsh-environment connectors
  • High-density, multi-pin round and rectangular connectors
  • High-reliability pogo-pin solutions for break-away and high mating cycles
  • PCB assembly (PCBA) for aerospace applications
  • System assembly and complete turnkey solutions
