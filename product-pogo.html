Products

Built for thousands
of mating cycles.

High-reliability pogo-pin solutions for break-away and high mating cycles – where a connector is engaged and released again and again, and still has to make the same contact on the last cycle as on the first.

Typical use

Docking, charging, test and break-away.

Spring-loaded contacts suit interfaces that are connected and disconnected constantly, and break-away designs protect both sides when a cable is pulled – a common requirement in medical carts, field equipment and test fixtures.

As with every connector family, we support the product from design and prototyping through to full-scale production, at whatever volume the program needs.
