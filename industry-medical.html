ARNIR specializes in the production of a wide range of medical cables, catheters, pre-amps, system cables, and connectors.

How we work

From concept, through design, to full mass production.

Medical products and systems are fully assembled in our factories with a focus on high quality, efficiency and sustainability.

We work closely with our partners from concept through design to full mass production, maintaining the highest standards with attention given to every detail.

By partnering with leading medical companies and staying informed about the latest trends and needs in the industry, we ensure that our products not only meet but exceed expectations.

ARNIR products are used worldwide in hospitals, operating rooms, emergency rooms, and homes.

What we build

Medical assemblies, end to end.

  • Medical cables and catheter assemblies
  • Pre-amps and system cables
  • Custom connectors, including disposable connectors
  • Electro-mechanical assemblies for medical devices
  • Full system and finished-product assembly under stringent QA
  • PCB assembly (PCBA) for medical applications
