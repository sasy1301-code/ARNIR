Services and Capabilities

Thirty years of assembling
taught us how to design.

ARNIR has been assembling cables for more than 30 years. Over the years, we succeeded in understanding the actual needs of our partners and focused on designing the cables, connectors and harnesses.

The process

Our experienced engineering team is dedicated
to the process, through all key steps involved.

  1. 01

    Requirements Gathering

    Initial Consultation – understanding specific needs, including application, environmental conditions, and performance expectations.

    Specification Development – documenting technical specifications, including electrical requirements, mechanical strength, environmental considerations, and connector and cable types.

  2. 02

    Material Selection

    We are researching and identifying the materials for conductors, insulation, and connectors.

  3. 03

    Design Planning

    Cable Architecture – creating a preliminary design outlining the structure, including the number and type of conductors, overall cable diameter, and insulation type.

    Electrical Design – evaluating the electrical characteristics, such as resistance, capacitance, and inductance, to ensure optimal performance for the intended application.

  4. 04

    Prototyping

    Tools Development – building the tools and the equipment necessary for future production.

    Prototype Development – building initial cable prototypes that reflect the planned designs.

    Testing – conducting testing to ensure they meet all specified operational requirements and industry standards.

    Compliance Certification – obtaining the necessary certifications for safety and performance.

By following this structured approach, we ensure that every cable, connector, and harness is designed to meet our partners’ expectations and is fully ready for mass production.
