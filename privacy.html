Privacy

What this site stores,
and why.

This site keeps as little as possible: the preferences you choose yourself, and – only if you agree to it – anonymous usage measurement that helps us improve the site.

What we store

Two kinds of storage, both in your browser.

  • Essential – your accessibility toolbar choices and your answer to the cookie notice
  • Statistics (optional) – anonymous measurement of which pages are visited, loaded only after you accept
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used anywhere on this site
  • Nothing you set in the accessibility toolbar ever leaves your device

We do not use advertising cookies, we do not sell information, and we do not pass it to third parties for marketing.

The details you enter in the contact form are used only to respond to your enquiry.

You can change or clear these preferences at any time – clearing this site’s data in your browser removes them entirely.

A question about privacy? Write to us at office@arnir.co.il.
