Capabilities

Boards built to the same
standard as the harness.

ARNIR provides PCB assembly for the military, medical and telecommunication industries – tailoring the process to each partner’s specific needs, from prototype runs to high-volume production.

Capabilities

Hybrid processes, inspection and test.

Our team uses technology and processes that ensure every PCB assembly we produce meets the highest standards. Whether low-volume production for prototypes or high-volume runs for mass production is required, our flexible manufacturing capabilities accommodate the requirement.

  • Hybrid processes – tin-lead and lead-free
  • Pin-through-hole, wave and selective soldering
  • Double- and single-sided reflow
  • Prototype assembly and PCB coating
  • Inspection with SPI, AOI and X-ray
  • The latest electrical testing equipment

Alongside

The board never travels alone.

Because the harness, the connector and the board are produced inside the same group, a populated board can move straight into cable integration and system assembly – without a hand-off to another supplier, and without a second qualification cycle.
