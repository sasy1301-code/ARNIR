Services and Capabilities

PCB assembly for the aerospace,
medical, and telecommunication industries.

ARNIR provides PCB assembly for the aerospace, medical, and telecommunication industries. Our team utilizes technology and processes to ensure that every PCB assembly we produce meets the highest standards.

Our capabilities

From a single prototype
to high-volume runs.

We tailor solutions to our partners’ specific needs. Whether low-volume production for prototypes or high-volume runs for mass production is required, our flexible manufacturing capabilities can accommodate the requirements.

Our capabilities include support for:

  • Hybrid processes (tin-lead and lead-free), pin through hole, wave & selective soldering, double and single-sided reflow
  • Prototype assembly and PCB coating
  • Testing and inspection using SPI, AOI, X-RAY, and the latest electrical testing equipment
