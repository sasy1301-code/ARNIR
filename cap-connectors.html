Services and Capabilities

Twenty years of
producing connectors.

ARNIR has been designing and producing the connectors in cooperation with Kenpol for more than 20 years. Our connectors are tailored to meet the varied demands of today’s fast-paced industries.

How we build

Advanced materials and precision machining.

By embracing advanced materials, technologies, and precision machining, we provide solutions and support, from design and prototyping to full-scale production.

ARNIR has a streamlined approach to production that maximizes resource utilization, ensures a reliable product, and delivers a cost-effective solution, all of which are crucial in today’s competitive market.

Our flexible production capabilities enable us to handle both large runs and smaller, specialized orders without compromising on quality or cost.

Product families

We offer a wide range of customized connectors for aerospace, medical, telecommunications, and industrial applications.

  • High-density, multi-pin round and rectangular connectors
  • High-reliability pogo pin solutions for brake-away and high mating cycles
  • Disposable connectors
  • RF connectors
  • Harsh Environment connectors
  • Composite materials, housings and insulator connectors
