What we build

Connectors and assemblies,
built to specification.

Every family below is designed in-house and produced across the group’s factories – from a single qualification batch to a multi-year production program.

Connector families

Four families, one design language.

High-density connectors

Multi-pin round and rectangular connectors for high signal density.

RF connectors & assemblies

RG, micro-coax, semi-flex and RF connectors.

Pogo-pin solutions

High reliability for break-away and high mating cycles.

Harsh-environment connectors

Vibration, temperature and humidity – including composite housings.

Also from us

Cables, harnesses and complete assemblies.

Beyond the connector itself, the group produces custom cable and harness assemblies, industrial power and molded cables, electrical looms and cabinet wiring, populated boards and fully assembled systems – so the whole interconnect chain can come from one supplier.
