Capabilities

Five factories.
Three hundred operators.

ARNIR specialises in the production of custom cable and harness assemblies for the military, medical and telecommunication industries – excelling in high-mix, low-volume work as well as mass production.

The floor

State-of-the-art machinery, certified operators.

Our factories are equipped with advanced machinery and tools for cutting, stripping, crimping, soldering, molding and electrical and mechanical testing. This lets us produce cable assemblies with exceptional accuracy and efficiency.

All members of our team are professionals who bring a wealth of knowledge and expertise, each contributing specialised skills to a polished final product. Our operators are IPC-620 certified and trained in comprehensive harness assembly techniques.

What we assemble

One assembly – or a million.

  • Custom cable assemblies and wire harnesses
  • RG, micro-coax, semi-flex and special RF assemblies
  • Industrial power cables and molded cables
  • Medical cables, catheters, pre-amps and system cables
  • Electrical looms, cabinet wiring and electro-mechanical integration
Each cable assembly is manufactured under stringent QA, to ensure that every product not only meets but exceeds industry standards.
