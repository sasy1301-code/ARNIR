Services and Capabilities

Five factories.
Three hundred operators.

ARNIR specializes in the production of custom cable and harness assemblies for the military, medical, and telecommunication industries. With five factories in Israel, Thailand, and India, and more than 300 operators, we excel in high-mix, low-volume, and mass production.

On the floor

State-of-the-art machinery,
an experienced team.

State-of-the-Art Machinery. Our factories are equipped with advanced machinery and tools for cutting, stripping, crimping, soldering, molding, and electrical and mechanical testing. This enables us to produce cable assemblies with exceptional accuracy and efficiency.

Experienced team. All members of our team are professionals who bring a wealth of knowledge and expertise to the table, each contributing their specialized skills to ensure a polished final product. Our operators are IPC620 certified and trained in comprehensive harness assembly techniques.

Quality and Certification

At the heart of our operations
is a commitment to quality.

Each cable assembly is manufactured under stringent QA to ensure that every product not only meets but exceeds industry standards.

All ARNIR factories are certified for: ISO 9001:2015, ISO 13485:2016, AS9100D.

Commitment to Customer Satisfaction. Customer satisfaction is the cornerstone of our business. We believe in establishing strong, long-term relationships with our partners. Our management team is dedicated to providing support at every stage of the process, from initial consultation to post-sale assistance.

The collaborative and adaptive assembly process allows us to work closely with our partners to create tailored solutions that align with their product requirements.
