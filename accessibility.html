Accessibility statement

A site everyone
can actually use.

ARNIR GROUP is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including visitors who use screen readers, keyboard-only navigation or magnification.

The toolbar

What the accessibility button does.

The round button at the bottom-left corner of every page opens an accessibility toolbar. Your choices are stored in your own browser and stay in place as you move between pages.

Nothing in the toolbar is sent anywhere – it only changes how this site is displayed on your device.

  • Text size – enlarge everything on the page in three steps, up to 135%
  • High contrast – a dark, high-contrast colour scheme
  • Highlight links – underlines every link so it can be told apart from text
  • Readable font – switches to a wider, more legible typeface
  • Stop animations – freezes every motion and transition on the site
  • Large cursor – an enlarged, high-contrast mouse pointer

How the site is built

Standards, keyboard and screen readers.

  • Every page can be operated with the keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline
  • A “Skip to content” link is the first item on every page
  • Headings follow a logical order, and images carry alternative text
  • Interactive controls expose their state to assistive technology
  • The site respects the operating system’s “reduce motion” setting
  • Colour is never the only way information is conveyed

The site is built to follow WCAG 2.1 level AA and Israeli Standard IS 5568. We test with keyboard navigation and screen readers, and fix issues as they are found.

Some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. We keep improving, and we would like to hear about any problem you run into.

Found an accessibility problem? Write to us at office@arnir.co.il and we will look into it.
