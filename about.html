About ARNIR GROUP

Established in 1993.
Five companies today.

ARNIR A.T. Communication 1993 Ltd. was established in 1993 in Israel. Initially founded as a cable harness assembly factory, ARNIR rapidly evolved and grew.

The story

From harness assembly
to design-in production.

In the late 2000s and the beginning of the 2010s, ARNIR started to specialize in design-in and build-to-spec production of connectors, cables, harnesses and electromechanical assemblies. During the same period, ARNIR executed the first acquisitions of small local companies, certified for ISO 13485 and AS 9100, and moved to a 3,000 SQM building in central Israel, reaching 120 employees.

During the 2010s, ARNIR developed a strong and unbreakable bond with the strategic Taiwanese supplier Kenpol Precision Technology Inc. The joint venture with Kenpol had a significant impact on ARNIR’s development over the years, and it continues to this day.

Growth

Every few years, another factory.

ARNIR continued working on the expansion and development of its capabilities – company by company, country by country.

  1. 2019

    Electo Galil Ltd.

    In 2019 ARNIR acquired Electo Galil Ltd in northern Israel. With a 2,700 SQM building, more than 100 employees, PCBA, military and medical cable harness manufacturing, and electro-mechanical assembly capabilities, Electo Galil was a significant reinforcement and essential extension of ARNIR’s portfolio.

  2. 2022

    FTK Solutions Ltd.

    ARNIR continued working on the expansion and development of the capabilities, and in 2022 acquired FTK Solutions Ltd. With the addition of another 2,000 SQM of production floor and the delivery of complete turnkey solutions in the military and industrial fields, ARNIR has become the leading interconnect design and production company in Israel.

  3. 2023

    ARNIR (Thailand) Ltd.

    In 2023, ARNIR (Thailand) Ltd. was established. The factory, located at Nava Nakhorn Industrial Zone near Bangkok, has over 3,000 square meters of production space and after only 10 months, reached 120 employees. Today, with the highest growth rate in the group, ARNIR (Thailand) Ltd. excels in medical and military cable harness production as well as electro-mechanical assembly for medical devices.

  4. 2024

    Trasccon Interconnection Systems Pvt Ltd.

    In 2024, one more company was added to ARNIR’s now already wide portfolio. A long-term supplier, Trasccon Interconnection Systems Pvt Ltd., joined the ARNIR family. The factory in Bangalore has 3,500 square meters of production floor and specializes in wire harnesses production, cable assemblies, electrical looms, cabinet wiring, and electromechanical integrations.
